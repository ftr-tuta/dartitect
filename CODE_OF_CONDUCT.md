# Code of Conduct

[Português (Brasil)](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community.
Be constructive, assume good faith while responding to evidence, respect
boundaries and privacy, and focus criticism on work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private/security information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the contact
method on their GitHub profiles; security vulnerabilities use `SECURITY.md`.
Maintainers may edit/remove content, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban participation according to impact and pattern. Retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project.
